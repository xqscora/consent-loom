Consent Loom

Turn a student's own words into the smallest support protocol a teacher can safely act on.

The problem

Students repeat sensitive support needs. Each retelling creates delay, over-collection, or an invented label. A useful system must preserve agency, not just predict a category.

The technical thesis

AI may propose structure, but it cannot authorize sharing. Provenance, expiry, revocation, and a deterministic policy checker are part of the model boundary.

From language to consent

1 · WriteStudent describes a situation and a possible support.
2 · ExtractAI proposes typed fields plus source spans.
3 · VerifyRules reject diagnosis, hidden recipients, and unbounded actions.
4 · ShareStudent confirms a minimum teacher view, then it expires or can be revoked.
